✈ MB339-Nav-Flightplan v3.0

MB339-Nav-Flightplan is a lightweight external utility developed in Python with a modern dark-avionics GUI, designed specifically for Microsoft Flight Simulator (2020 & 2024) and tailored for the IndiaFoxtEcho Aermacchi MB-339.

🌐 English / Italiano

🚀 Key Features (EN)

Dual Route Loading: Fetch your active flight plan directly from SimBrief or load any local .pln file. Your SimBrief Pilot ID is now securely saved locally (.json) with auto-masking for privacy (ideal for streamers).

Advanced Telemetry Panel (NEW): Real-time monitoring of essential flight data: TAS (True Airspeed), GS (Ground Speed), ALT (Altitude), WIND (Direction/Velocity), and MACH number.

Avionics LED Display & Pro-Nav: Dynamic, expansive digital display showing the active waypoint, heading bearing (BEARING), and precise distance (DISTANCE NM). Now features live ETE (Estimated Time Enroute), EFOB (Estimated Fuel On Board in KG), and XTK (Cross-Track Error).

Smart Auto-Sync & Reverse Route (NEW): Automatically detects your aircraft's position upon connection and selects the correct active leg. Need to fly back? Reverse the entire flight plan with a single click!

Native MSFS Bridge (SimConnect): Continuously monitors your aircraft's GPS coordinates and automatically steers the autopilot Heading Bug along your route, sequencing to the next waypoint when within 0.8 NM.

Discord Integration: Direct in-app access to the SKYSIM PILOT community server.

Bilingual UI: Instantly switch between English and Italian at any time with dedicated flag buttons.

🚀 Caratteristiche Principali (IT)

Doppia Modalità Rotta: Importa il piano di volo da SimBrief o carica qualsiasi file .pln. L'ID SimBrief viene ora salvato localmente per i successivi riavvii e mascherato per la privacy (perfetto per gli streamer).

Pannello Telemetria Avanzata (NOVITÀ): Monitoraggio in tempo reale dei dati di volo essenziali: TAS (True Airspeed), GS (Ground Speed), ALT (Altitudine), VENTO (Direzione/Intensità) e numero di MACH.

Display LED Avionico & Pro-Nav: Pannello digitale espansivo con waypoint attivo, prua (BEARING) e distanza (DISTANCE NM). Ora include calcoli live per ETE (Tempo Stimato all'Arrivo), EFOB (Carburante Stimato in KG) e XTK (Errore di Fuori Rotta / Cross-Track).

Auto-Sync Intelligente & Inversione Rotta (NOVITÀ): Rileva automaticamente la posizione dell'aereo alla connessione e sincronizza il waypoint corretto. Volo di ritorno? Inverti l'intero piano di volo con un singolo clic!

Ponte Nativo MSFS (SimConnect): Monitora costantemente la posizione GPS e pilota automaticamente l'Heading Bug dell'autopilota, passando al waypoint successivo a 0.8 NM.

Integrazione Discord: Accesso istantaneo al server Discord della community SKYSIM PILOT direttamente dall'app.

Interfaccia Bilingue: Selettore rapido integrato tra Inglese e Italiano.

📋 System Requirements / Requisiti

Microsoft Flight Simulator (MSFS 2020 or MSFS 2024)

Aermacchi MB-339 (IndiaFoxtEcho)

Windows 10 / 11

📦 Installation / Installazione

Download the .zip archive.

Extract the MB339-Nav-Flightplan.exe anywhere on your computer.

Run the executable while MSFS is open.

✈️ Cockpit Operating Instructions (MB-339)

Autopilot Setup: Turn on electrical power and avionics. Ensure the Autopilot is engaged (Master ON) and press the HDG (Heading) button on the glareshield panel. Engage ALT HOLD for cruise altitude.

Load Route: Enter your SimBrief Pilot ID and click Fetch OFP, or click Load .PLN to open a local flight plan.

Connect: Click Connect MSFS to establish SimConnect communication.

Start Navigation: Click START NAV (HDG) and enjoy your flight! The system will automatically sequence to the next waypoint when within 0.8 NM.
